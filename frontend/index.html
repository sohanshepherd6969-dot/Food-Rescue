How FoodRescue Works

A simple way to connect surplus food with people who need it.

List Surplus Food

Hostels, restaurants, caterers or individuals post available food with details and a photo.

Find & Claim

Distributors and volunteer groups browse nearby listings by area and claim a pickup.

Collect Food

Coordinate pickup with the donor before the deadline and collect the food.

Distribute

Share the food with communities in need and rate the pickup to build trust.

About FoodRescue

Every day, hostels, restaurants and event caterers in Bangalore cook more than they serve. FoodRescue gives that surplus a short, safe path to people who need it.

  • Donors post surplus food with quantity, food type, area and a pickup deadline.
  • Distributors filter by area, price and food type, then claim and collect.
  • Food is free or nominally priced: Free, ₹5 or ₹10 per plate, never more.
  • An admin team moderates listings and handles reports.
